# §17 — Chapter 33: Complementary and Alternative Therapies

> Chapter ledger entry, relocated from `goldenversion.md`. Historical record — read only if
> you need this chapter's detailed rationale.

**Content classification.** Ch33 (Shen, Chen) is almost entirely **Bucket B** (clinical judgment/decision content). There are no quantifiable physiology formulas (Bucket A) and no procedural technique content (Bucket C). Its value is in identifying outpatient herbal and dietary supplement usage, determining perioperative risks (bleeding, sedation, cardiovascular, hepatic, immune, enzyme induction, withdrawal), managing appropriate discontinuation timelines, and understanding mind-body practices (acupuncture, music therapy, deep breathing).

#### 17.1 Structured CAM Knowledge Layer (`CAMKnowledgeEngine.ts`)

A pure TypeScript data layer directly mapping the clinical details of Miller Chapter 33:
*   **`HERBAL_MEDICINES`**: Constant array representing all 11 herbs from Table 33.1 (Echinacea, Ephedra, Garlic, Ginger, Ginkgo, Ginseng, Green Tea, Kava, Saw Palmetto, St. John's Wort, Valerian) with scientific names, uses, effects, concerns, concern types, and drug interactions.
*   **`DIETARY_SUPPLEMENTS`**: Sourced data for Fish Oil, Coenzyme Q10, and Glucosamine/Chondroitin including warfarin interactions.
*   **`CAM_THERAPIES`**: Sourced details for P6 acupuncture/acupressure (PONV prevention evidence, timing), music therapy (sedative requirement reduction), and slow deep breathing (vagal activation, pain relief).
*   **`assessHerbalRisks(herbs, dietary)`**: Pure evaluation function returning categorized risk lists and a textual summary.
*   **`getDiscontinuationGuidance(herbId)`**: Pure function providing discontinuation intervals (e.g. 7 days for garlic/ginseng, 36 hours/2 days for ginkgo, 24 hours/1 day for ephedra/kava, 2 weeks/14 days for ginger and dietary supplements) and safety notes for Echinacea/Valerian.

#### 17.2 PreOp EMR Section 5 — Herbal & CAM Assessment (`PreOpEMR.jsx`)

Surfaced in the EMR's Risk Assessment tab as a new Section 5 card following the exact visual style and checklist paradigm:
*   **Vignette**: Displays the patient's herbal history (`groundTruth.herbalHistory`) showing active continued outpatient CAM therapies.
*   **Interactive Screening**: Checklist requiring user verification ("Yes, herbal/supplement screening performed"). If omitted, `verifyAssessment()` throws a validation error.
*   **Educational Disclosure**: If the patient is taking herbs, confirming screening reveals Ch33-derived concerns, discontinuation guidance, and active interaction risk banners.
*   **Outpatient Disclosure Card**: Added to the EMR's Chart tab Medications section (beneath Ch31/Ch32 cards), listing all outpatient herbs/supplements, scientific names, effects, and color-coded warning badges (🔴 Bleeding Risk, 🟡 Sedation Synergy, 🟠 CYP3A4 Inducer).

#### 17.3 Quality-of-Care Scoring Hooks (`usePhysiology.js`)

Six new quality-of-care audit checks are integrated into the main tick loop, logging events to `OutcomeScoringEngine` when specific care thresholds are missed or met:
1.  **Herbal screening omission** (`ChecklistAdherence`, `moderate`): Fired if patient is taking active outpatient CAM therapies and surgery starts without PreOp screening verification.
2.  **Valerian/Kava + sedative synergy** (`PharmacologicChoice`, `minor`): Fired if valerian/kava are active in system while standard doses of propofol or midazolam are administered.
3.  **St. John's Wort CYP3A4 induction** (`PharmacologicChoice`, `moderate`): Fired if St. John's Wort is active while CYP3A4 substrates (midazolam, lidocaine, fentanyl, alfentanil) are given.
4.  **Bleeding risk herbs + neuraxial** (`Vigilance`, `major`): Fired if neuraxial anesthesia is attempted while active antiplatelet herbal/dietary supplements (garlic, ginkgo, ginseng, ginger, fish oil) are in system.
5.  **Ephedra + volatile anesthetic** (`CrisisManagement`, `major`): Fired if ephedra is present while inhalational anesthetics (sevoflurane/desflurane/isoflurane/halothane) are administered, warning of cardiac sensitization.
6.  **P6 stimulation credit** (`PharmacologicChoice`, `info`): Fired if P6 acupressure is applied before anesthesia induction as PONV prophylaxis.

#### 17.4 Clinical Actions and Patient State (`App.jsx`, `ClinicalActions.jsx`)

*   **`patient.herbalSupplements` / `dietarySupplements`**: Registered in the default patient state tree and preset cases (e.g. `normal` preset has `['garlic', 'valerian']` and `['fishOil']`).
*   **"Apply P6 Acupressure"**: Custom clinical action (`apply_p6`) sets `patient.p6StimulationApplied = true` and logs the clinical action to the console.
*   **"Ask About Herbal Medicines"**: Custom clinical action (`ask_herbal`) sets `patient.herbalScreeningDone = true` pre-induction.

#### 17.5 What Was Not Integrated This Session

Ch33 includes extensive details on the biochemical mechanisms of acupuncture (fMRI anterior insula signal increases, periaqueductal gray and arcuate neuron activation pathways), legal and regulatory history of dietary supplements (DSHEA 1994, lack of FDA pre-market safety reviews), and qualitative descriptions of yoga or massage therapy. These clinical background details and qualitative descriptions were **not integrated** into the simulator state because they do not map to active physiological simulations or direct perioperative scoring, remaining in the Attending chat background context instead.
