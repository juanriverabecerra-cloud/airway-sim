**Where this chapter's content actually lives** (not duplicated here — see the linked sections):
*   **Dantrolene PK/PD** — `docs/engines/pharmacology.md` §5.10 (medication data table): $V_1 = 15.0\text{ L}$, $k_{10} = 0.00115$, $ke_0 = 0.1$, $EC_{50} = 1.5\text{ mcg/mL}$. *   **Malignant Hyperthermia (MH) Crisis Loop** — `docs/engines/clinical_events.md` §6.81: trigger conditions, hypermetabolic cascade (temperature/CO2/lactate/K+/HR), charcoal-filter circuit scrubbing, Dantrolene rescue, and the three quality-of-care event hooks (MHS triggering event, delayed Dantrolene, lethal CCB+Dantrolene interaction).
*   **Neuromuscular Disease Sensitivity Rules** — `docs/engines/clinical_events.md` §6.82: Myasthenia Gravis, Charcot-Marie-Tooth, Eaton-Lambert, Critical Illness Polyneuropathy, Mitochondrial Myopathy, and Duchenne/Becker Muscular Dystrophy sensitivity multipliers and succinylcholine hyperkalemia interactions.
*   **Myasthenia Gravis Postoperative Ventilation Risk Scorecard** — `docs/engines/clinical_events.md` §6.83: the 9-factor Lee/Miller Box 35.8 scoring system (vital capacity, disease duration, pyridostigmine dose, pulmonary disease, bulbar symptoms, prior crisis, surgical blood loss, antibody titer, decremental EMG), classifying Low/Intermediate/High postoperative ventilation risk. Implemented as the exported `calculateMyastheniaPostopVentRisk(patient, caseId)` in `PreOpEMR.jsx`, surfaced in the Risk Assessment tab.
