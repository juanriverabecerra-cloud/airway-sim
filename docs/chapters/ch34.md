# §18 — Chapter 34: Patient Positioning and Associated Risks

> Chapter ledger entry, relocated from `goldenversion.md`. Historical record — read only if
> you need this chapter's detailed rationale.

**Content classification.** Ch34 (Prielipp, Warner) is a cross-cutting chapter covering **Bucket A** (quantifiable positioning hemodynamics, compliance and preload adjustments, inferior vena cava [IVC] compression penalties), **Bucket B** (clinical screening in PreOp EMR, closed claims peripheral nerve injury [PNI] statistics, Table 34.4 POVL odds ratios, and laryngeal/facial edema after prolonged head-down positioning), and **Bucket C** (supine-to-prone logroll steps and anatomical landmarks represented as structured data for future procedural engine expansion).

#### 18.1 Structured Positioning Knowledge Layer (`PositioningKnowledgeEngine.ts`)

A pure TypeScript data layer directly mapping the clinical details of Miller Chapter 34:
*   **`POSITIONS_DATA`**: Constant array representing all 7 surgical positions (Supine, Trendelenburg, Reverse Trendelenburg, Prone, Lateral, Lithotomy, Sitting) with Map/CO/preload changes, compliance, FRC changes, and cardiorespiratory summaries.
*   **`NERVES_DATA`**: Table 34.1 peripheral nerve injuries (ulnar 14%, brachial plexus 19%, sciatic/peroneal 7%, spinal cord 25%) with closed claims percentages, mechanisms, risk factors, and prevention steps.
*   **`POVL_DATA`**: Table 34.4 risk factors and odds ratios for perioperative visual loss during spine surgery (Male sex OR 2.53, Obesity OR 2.83, Wilson frame OR 4.30, Anesthesia duration OR 1.39/hr, Blood loss OR 1.34/L, Colloid ratio OR 0.67 per 5%).
*   **`PROCEDURAL_GROUNDWORK`**: Structured landmarks, steps, and complications for maneuvers (Supine-to-Prone Logroll, Lithotomy).
*   **`checkPovlRisk(patient, durationHrs, eblL, colloidPct, useWilsonFrame)`**: Pure evaluation function calculating POVL risk category, score, and odds ratio.

#### 18.2 Physiology Engines Integration (`RespiratoryEngine.ts`, `usePhysiology.js`)

*   **Abdominal/Visceral Compression Penalty**: In `RespiratoryEngine.ts`, if the patient is in `Prone` position without proper supports (`!safePatient.proneSupportsPlaced`), a 30% reduction in respiratory compliance is applied to simulate visceral elevation against the diaphragm.
*   **Preload / Venous Return Penalty**: In `usePhysiology.js`, the prone preload offset is adjusted from a minor -100 mL drop to a severe -350 mL drop if supports are not placed (`!st.patient.proneSupportsPlaced`), representing compression on the inferior vena cava (IVC).
*   **Post-Extubation Laryngeal Edema**: In `usePhysiology.js`, if `patient.postExtubationLaryngealEdema` is true (triggered on extubation after prolonged head-down positioning without a cuff leak test), the `airwayObstructionIndex` is set to a floor of `0.8`, triggering severe airway resistance, hypoxia, and desaturation.

#### 18.3 PreOp EMR Section 6 — Patient Positioning Risk Assessment (`PreOpEMR.jsx`)

Surfaced in the EMR's Risk Assessment tab as a new Section 6 card:
*   **Vignette**: Displays the planned surgical position and estimated duration.
*   **Interactive Screening**: Checklist requiring user verification ("Yes, positioning risk assessment performed"). If omitted, `verifyAssessment()` throws a validation error.
*   **Educational Disclosure**: Confirming screening reveals Ch34-derived physiology details, vulnerable nerves, and POVL risk details.
*   **Verification Checklist**: Expanded the EMR verification checklist to 8 items, including "Positioning".

#### 18.4 Quality-of-Care Scoring Hooks (`usePhysiology.js`, `App.jsx`)

Six quality-of-care audit checks log events to the `OutcomeScoringEngine`:
1.  **Positioning screening omission** (`ChecklistAdherence`, `moderate`): Fired if surgery starts without PreOp positioning screening verification.
2.  **Brachial plexus / ulnar nerve injury risk** (`Vigilance`, `minor`): Fired in Supine/Prone position during active incision/maintenance if arms are not padded or positioned correctly.
3.  **Peroneal nerve compression risk** (`Vigilance`, `moderate`): Fired in Lithotomy during active surgery if stirrups/legs are unpadded.
4.  **Prolonged Lithotomy compartment syndrome risk** (`Vigilance`, `major`): Fired if lithotomy time exceeds 120 minutes without leg lowering for reperfusion.
5.  **Prone POVL vigilance omission** (`Vigilance`, `major`): Fired during prolonged prone procedures (>120 minutes) if face/eye checks are neglected for >20 minutes.
6.  **Post-obstructive laryngeal edema** (`Vigilance`, `moderate` / `info`): Fired in `App.jsx` `handleExtubation()` if the patient spent >240 minutes in head-down (Trendelenburg/Prone) position. Fired as `moderate` (and sets `postExtubationLaryngealEdema: true` and `airwayObstructionIndex: 0.8`) if no cuff leak test was checked, or `info` if cuff leak was successfully checked.

#### 18.5 Clinical Actions and Patient State (`App.jsx`, `ClinicalActions.jsx`)

*   **State Additions**: `positioningAssessmentDone`, `armsPositionedCorrectly`, `peronealNervePadded`, `proneSupportsPlaced`, `lastLegsLoweredTime`, `legsLoweredCount`, `lastHeadEyeCheckTime`, `headEyeCheckCount`, `postExtubationLaryngealEdema`.
*   **New Actions**: Registered positioning actions (`ask_positioning`, `pad_arms`, `pad_legs`, `place_prone_supports`, `lower_legs`, `check_head_eyes`).
*   **New Case Preset**: Added `spine_prone` case preset representing an obese patient undergoing a 7-hour lumbar spine fusion, creating a high-risk scenario for both IVC compression and POVL.

#### 18.6 What Was Not Integrated This Session

Ch34 details historical positioning chairs (e.g. historical sitting tables), detailed mechanical properties of specific chest roll manufacturers, and legal outcomes of specific closed claims lawsuits. These historical and mechanical background details were **not integrated** into the simulator state because they do not map to active physiological simulations or direct perioperative scoring, remaining in the Attending chat background context instead.
