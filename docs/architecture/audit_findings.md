- *Delivery census* — administer each drug at its indication dose and confirm effect-site Ce/A1 rises:
  **172/173 deliver** (ziconotide's 0.1 mcg intrathecal dose is legitimately sub-threshold, not broken;
  0 non-administrable — the earlier `giveMed` camelCase-key bug is fixed).

  assert the sim moves that vital in the declared direction. Real gaps found: **F26 isoproterenol**
  (fixed). The rest of the initial "fails" were verified as census artifacts (slow-onset drugs at a
  too-short horizon — metoprolol/digoxin already have passing 240-step laws), correct physiology
  (hemorrhage doesn't change Hb *concentration*; high-dose epinephrine → reflex bradycardia), or the
  niche cluster **F29**. Non-hemodynamic drugs (antibiotics, anticoagulants, antiemetics, anticonvulsants,
  reversal agents) have no `pd` hemodynamics by design; their key effects (glucose, reversal, TOF, BIS)
  are covered by the dedicated direction-law batteries.

**Fluid/transfusion census (L2, all 9 `FLUIDS`, `fluid_effects_ch2.test.ts`):** every fluid registered on
the real FluidicsEngine path. After **F32**: PRBC raises Hb while every red-cell-free product
